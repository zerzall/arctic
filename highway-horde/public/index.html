    How to Play

    The goal

    Waves of zombies pour in from the edges of the map. Defend the objective in the middle — the school bus, the diner, the APC or the radio tower — and survive every wave. If the objective is destroyed, or nobody is left standing, the horde wins.

    Evac Run (pick it under Mode in the lobby; the big Harlan County map is made for it): every wave the safe zone moves to a new spot on the map. A countdown tells you where and how long you have — follow the teal ring on the compass and the minimap, grab the supply drop waiting inside, fight off the stragglers on the way. When the wave starts the circle locks, later it shrinks to a smaller one; outside it the blight eats your health faster and faster (armour doesn't help), so stay inside. Press N once you're in the zone to start the wave early.

    Night or day: the Time row in the lobby switches every map between the original moonlit night (flashlights, lamps and fires) and a sunny day with long shadows and haze. It changes how it looks, not how it plays; some maps only exist in one of them.

    Campaign (pick it under Mode; it plays on Checkpoint Delta, Highway 9 Pileup and Harlan County, in daylight): four stages in one game. Hilltop stand — hold a fortified hill: the slope slows the horde and they take extra damage, and you deal a bonus from the high ground. Breakout — the hill is overrun: fight along the street to the tall tower while a red horde front walks behind you; anyone it catches takes escalating damage. The ascent — clear the lobby, the offices and the atrium floor by floor; when a floor is clear the stairs open. Rooftop — kill the quota, then ride the zip line (E at the gantry) down to the landing pad. Everyone still alive must escape to win.

    First person: click the game to capture the mouse, move the mouse to look, Esc gives the mouse back. The compass at the top points to the objective (◆) and the supply station (+). Prefer the old bird's-eye view? Pick Classic top-down in Settings → Controls.

    Big screen: hit Fullscreen on the title screen, in the pause menu or in Settings. Esc gives the mouse back and opens the menu; in Chrome and Edge fullscreen stays on (hold Esc to leave it). Settings → Graphics picks the quality preset, Display & HUD the UI size.

    Survival tips

    • Revive your friends. A downed player bleeds out in 30 seconds. Stand next to them and hold E — you earn cash for every revive. Dead players come back when the wave is cleared.
    • Spend your cash. Kills pay. Between waves the shop is open everywhere; during a wave only at the supply station (green + on the minimap and the compass) — in an Evac Run also at the zone's supply drop.
    • Campaign: stay on the high ground. On the hill, stand near the top: the horde is slowed on the flank and you hit harder. When the breakout starts, don't stop to fight: keep ahead of the red front. On the roof, spread the kills, revive fast and ride together.
    • Evac Run: move early. Leave for the next zone right away: the stragglers only get thicker, and the circle doesn't wait. Stay near its middle when it shrinks.
    • Watch your back. The horde comes from every side: turn when you hear groans behind you (they sound muffled), and follow the red arcs that show where a hit came from.
    • Bosses every 5 waves. The Abomination slams the ground around it — keep your distance and focus fire.
    • Know the specials. Bloaters burst when they die, spitters lob acid, screamers speed up the horde, brutes charge.
    • Jump the low stuff. Space hops over guard rails, jersey barriers, sandbags and fences (not barricades) — sprint for a longer leap. Crawlers can't reach you in the air.
    • Climb for a better view. Space while walking into a car, van, HESCO wall or container pulls you up on top — shoot over the cover around you, hop roof to roof, walk off the edge to get down. It buys time, not safety: zombies grab your legs on a car, runners and walkers climb after you, brutes knock you off.
    • Fortify. Barricades make zombies go around or smash through; sentry turrets shoot on their own.
    • Play your class. Doc heals and revives fast, Sparks builds turrets, Boom loves explosions, Tank soaks hits, Swift outruns everything, Sarge just shoots better.
